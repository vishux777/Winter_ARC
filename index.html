How to run 12 sessions responsibly

Session length~45 minutes
Between sessions~10 hours
Warm-up5–7 min + ramp-up sets
Working setsUsually 1–3 reps in reserve
Heavy compound rest2–3 min
Accessories rest45–90 sec
Rest dayTuesday — full rest
Important: 12 resistance sessions per week is a demanding schedule, even with a 10-hour gap. This plan intentionally keeps several sessions low-volume and avoids failure. Start the first 2 weeks at about 60–70% of listed working sets if you're not already adapted to this frequency. Do not add extra sets or finishers.
If strength drops across several sessions, soreness lasts more than 48–72 hours, sleep worsens, or joints/tendons hurt, reduce volume or remove the evening session temporarily. Aim for 7–9 hours of sleep and regular meals between sessions.
Cardio is short and mostly low-impact: bike or elliptical at a pace where you can still speak in sentences. Running is optional and should wait until outdoor air quality is suitable. If you have a medical condition or persistent pain, get professional guidance before following a high-frequency plan.
